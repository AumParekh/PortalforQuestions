\chapterhead{51}{ORR-12}{Guidance on Managing Outsourcing Risk}{1}{3}

\lohead{51}{a}{Explain how risks can arise through outsourcing activities to
third-party service providers and describe elements of an effective program to
manage outsourcing risk.}

\orsub{Risks of outsourcing activities to third-party service providers}

Financial institutions can benefit from outsourcing tasks, but it also introduces
risks.

\noindent\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.35}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{0.22\textwidth}Y@{}}
\rowcolor{navy} \thd{Risk} & \thd{How it arises}\\
{\tabfont \textbf{Compliance risk}} & {\tabfont The service provider might not follow regulations.}\\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
{\tabfont \textbf{Concentration risk}} & {\tabfont Relying on too few providers, or having them concentrated geographically, can be risky.}\\
{\tabfont \textbf{Reputational risk}} & {\tabfont Poor service by a provider can damage public image.}\\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
{\tabfont \textbf{Country risk}} & {\tabfont Using a provider in a risky country exposes you to economic and political instability.}\\
{\tabfont \textbf{Operational risk}} & {\tabfont The provider's mistakes or internal control failures could cause losses.}\\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
{\tabfont \textbf{Legal risk}} & {\tabfont The provider's negligence might lead to lawsuits against the company.}\\
\end{tabularx}
\end{minipage}
\orfigcap{The six risk channels opened by outsourcing.}

\orsub{Effective program to manage outsourcing risk}

\begin{lettered}
\item \textbf{Risk assessments.} Carefully evaluate business activities to
determine if outsourcing is the best option. This includes analysing whether
qualified service providers exist and if the institution has the capacity to
oversee the relationship effectively.
\item \textbf{Due diligence.} Thoroughly research potential service providers,
assessing their experience, reputation, and security practices.
\end{lettered}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
\lohead{51}{b}{Explain how financial institutions should perform due diligence on
third-party service providers.}

\orsub{Due diligence on service providers}

\orsubb{Business background and strategy}
\begin{checks}
\item Assess the provider's history, management, and operational model.
\item Confirm employee background checks, licenses, and certifications.
\item Investigate any legal or compliance issues.
\end{checks}

\orsubb{Financial performance}
\begin{checks}
\item Review the financial health of any subcontractors.
\item Analyse financial statements to evaluate stability and liquidity.
\end{checks}

\orsubb{Operations and internal controls}
\begin{checks}
\item Evaluate internal controls, IT security, and data protection measures.
\item Check staff training and support services.
\item Review disaster recovery plans and record-keeping processes.
\end{checks}

\lohead{51}{c}{Describe topics and provisions that should be addressed in a
contract with a third-party service provider.}

\orsub{Contract provisions}

\noindent\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{0.27\textwidth}Y@{}}
\rowcolor{navy} \thd{Provision} & \thd{What it must address}\\
{\tabfont \textbf{1) Scope of work}} &
{\tabfont Define rights and responsibilities, contract duration, support,
maintenance, training, subcontracting policies, insurance, and use of the
financial institution's assets and employees.}\\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
{\tabfont \textbf{2) Costs and compensation}} &
{\tabfont Detail payment responsibilities, including for equipment, legal, and
audit fees; list all compensation types (fixed, variable, special charges).}\\
{\tabfont \textbf{3) Incentive compensation}} &
{\tabfont Include terms to review incentive structures, ensuring they prioritise
customer interests and do not introduce excessive risk.}\\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
{\tabfont \textbf{4) Right to audit}} &
{\tabfont Optionally include audit rights for the financial institution and
requirements for the service provider to supply audit reports at defined
intervals.}\\
{\tabfont \textbf{5) Performance standards}} &
{\tabfont State specific, measurable standards and metrics for the service
provider's performance.}\\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
{\tabfont \textbf{6) Oversight and monitoring}} &
{\tabfont Require annual financial statements from the provider to monitor
ongoing viability; stipulate increased oversight for underperforming or high-risk
providers.}\\
{\tabfont \textbf{7) Confidentiality and security}} &
{\tabfont Include provisions on information confidentiality and security, data
access, and breach notification, ensuring compliance with privacy laws.}\\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
{\tabfont \textbf{8) Ownership and licensing}} &
{\tabfont Clarify ownership of data and control of materials produced; potentially
include escrow agreements for software source code.}\\
{\tabfont \textbf{9) Indemnification}} &
{\tabfont Require the service provider to indemnify the financial institution
against legal issues arising from the provider's negligence.}\\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
{\tabfont \textbf{10) Default and termination}} &
{\tabfont Define actions constituting default, remedies, and procedures for
termination, including data and property return.}\\
{\tabfont \textbf{11) Dispute resolution}} &
{\tabfont Outline a plan for resolving disputes to minimise service disruption.}\\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
{\tabfont \textbf{12) Limits on liability}} &
{\tabfont Allow service providers to limit their liability, subject to board and
management approval.}\\
{\tabfont \textbf{13) Insurance}} &
{\tabfont Mandate that service providers carry sufficient insurance and provide
proof of coverage.}\\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
{\tabfont \textbf{14) Customer complaints}} &
{\tabfont Assign responsibility for handling customer complaints and reporting to
the financial institution.}\\
{\tabfont \textbf{15) Business resumption and contingency}} &
{\tabfont Detail the service provider's disaster recovery and business continuity
plans, including maintenance and testing frequencies.}\\
\rowcolor{paleblue}
{\tabfont \textbf{16) Foreign-based providers}} &
{\tabfont Ensure the contract stipulates that the laws of the financial
institution's jurisdiction govern contract enforcement and dispute resolution.}\\
{\tabfont \textbf{17) Subcontracting}} &
{\tabfont Hold the primary service provider accountable for the work of
subcontractors, outlining acceptable subcontracted tasks, supervision, and due
diligence processes.}\\
\end{tabularx}
\end{minipage}
